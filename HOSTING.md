# Quick launch checklist

1. Open `index.html` locally and click through all five pages.
2. Check both videos on desktop and mobile.
3. Add your best portrait and BTS photos to the Gallery page.
4. Replace any temporary project imagery with official stills you have permission to use.
5. If the site feels slow, host the two large MP4s on Vimeo and embed them instead.
6. Upload the folder to Netlify or Vercel.
7. Connect a custom domain.
8. Test the final public link on phone, laptop and incognito mode.
9. Add the public URL to your CV, LinkedIn, email signature and applications.
